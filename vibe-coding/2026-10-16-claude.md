**Sidebar not restoring.** I tested GTK 4.14 directly, and the cause is in how the app reads GTK's signals:
- When the sidebar list is rebuilt, GTK tears down every row. Each one reports "collapsed" while still pointing at its folder.
- The app took each of those as you collapsing the folder, so it forgot them all and saved that to your config.
- The background scan does a rebuild right after launch. So the tree restored, your photos loaded, and then everything collapsed while the photos stayed up.

The fix ignores collapses from torn-down rows; a real collapse is still remembered. On launch the app now also opens every parent of your last folder, selects it and scrolls it into view. That means it works even with the config the bug already saved. Collapsing a folder now also forgets the folders open inside it, so they don't spring back open later.
