**What happens now.**

- **Switching folders mid-load:** the old folder's pending work is dropped, so it never competes with the new one.
- **During a scan:** the grid of the open folder now only adds slots for new photos and removes deleted ones. Your selection, scroll position and loaded thumbnails stay put.
